Privacy

How we handle your information

Short version: we only use what you give us to reach out about TBT, and we never sell or share it with third parties. Here's the fuller picture.

What we collect

When you fill out a sign-up form (to join TBT, update your info, sign up for a Quad or Home Group, or request prayer), we collect whatever you enter — typically your name, phone number, email, and any message you include. These forms are hosted by Planning Center's Church Center platform, not directly on this website.

How we use it

We use your information only to communicate with you about TBT — following up on a form, adding you to a group, or responding to a request. That's it. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't sell or share it with third-party marketers.

Texting us directly

If you text one of our team numbers, that message goes to a real person on our team — not an automated system — and is used only to have that conversation with you.

Prayer wall

On the prayer wall we store what you type: a first name or "Anonymous," your request, and when you sent it. Requests you share on the wall are public and come off the wall after 30 days. Requests marked team only are not shown on this site, and only Pastor Bruce and the TBT team can read them. We don't ask for your email, phone number, or last name here, and we ask you not to include them. Requests are kept in a Google Sheet that the TBT team controls. To have a request removed, text us at (234) 297-7413 or email bchimani@oldnorthchurch.org.

Cookies & tracking

This site does not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising trackers.

Questions

If you have any questions about your information, text us at (234) 297-7413 or reach out through Old North Church.
