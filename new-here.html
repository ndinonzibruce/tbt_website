New Here?

Whatever brought you to campus this year, we'd love to meet you.

The Bible Talks (TBT) is a campus ministry at YSU — students reading the Bible, asking honest questions, and doing life together. Freshman, transfer, grad student, or just new to TBT — you don't need an invitation. Reach out for any reason below, and someone from our team will get back to you.

More about who we are

Grab coffee

No agenda. Just a conversation with someone who's been where you are.

Make a friend

Starting over socially is hard. We'll help you find people.

Talk about Jesus

Whether you're curious, skeptical, or ready to dive in — we want to talk.

Let us know a need

Ride to the store, someone to pray with, a rough week — tell us. We mean it.

Need a ride somewhere

Groceries, an appointment, the airport — if you're stuck without a car, ask.

Ask a question about the Bible

No question is too basic or too hard. We'd genuinely like to talk it through.

Ask for prayer

Something specific going on? Tell us and we'll pray for it — with you or on our own.

Visit a Sunday service

Don't want to walk in alone? We'll meet you there and sit with you.

Reach out — it's easy

Text a real person on our team directly, or fill out a 30-second form and we'll follow up personally.

Text Our Team: (234) 297-7413 Introduce Yourself

We'll only use your info to reach out about TBT — we never share it with third parties.

Not ready to reach out yet? Just show up — Mondays, 4pm, Williamson Building, Room 2212. No text required.
