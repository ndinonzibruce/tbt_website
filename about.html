About

What is The Bible Talks?

The Bible Talks is a campus ministry at Youngstown State University. We gather students each week to read the Bible together, pray for one another, and talk honestly about what we find there.

Our conviction is simple: God speaks through open Bibles. So we don't build our meetings around performance or personality. We open the text, work through it a book at a time, and let it shape how we live on campus and after we leave it.

You don't need to have read the Bible before, and you don't need to have your beliefs sorted out. Bring your questions.
